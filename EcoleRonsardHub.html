About EcoleRonsardHub

EcoleRonsardHub is an educational dashboard designed to help students learn, practise and test themselves.

The quiz system supports Grades 1–12 and generates different questions for each grade and subject.

Subjects: Mathematics, English, Science, French, Geography, History, Computing and Business.
